Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about learning, packages, payments and support.

Before purchasing: Read the package details, applicable Terms & Conditions and Refund Policy. Work availability and earnings are not guaranteed.

Learning & packages

What is Smart Money?
Smart Money is a digital learning and skill-development platform. It provides learning paths and information about practical digital skills. Review each package page for its current lessons and access details.
How do I compare packages?
Compare the displayed price, course list, features and access conditions on the relevant package page. If any information appears inconsistent, contact support before making a payment.
Does buying a package guarantee a job or income?
No. A package does not guarantee employment, client assignments, continuous tasks or a particular income. Eligible work may be available depending on the actual opportunities and written task instructions.

Payments & activation

When does package access begin?
Access begins after the payment and any required account or administrator checks are successfully verified. A bank message alone does not always mean the Smart Money account has been activated.
What should I do if payment is pending?
Do not immediately pay again. Check the transaction status and contact support with the transaction reference and approximate payment time. Never send a password, OTP, banking PIN or full card details.

Refund commitment

How does the 30-day ₹0 earning refund commitment work?
The 30-day period starts from package activation, not purchase. If no eligible Smart Money work earnings are credited during the first 30 calendar days after activation, the user may request the published 100% package-fee refund, subject to verification and the full Refund Policy.
What counts as earning?
The commitment uses amounts actually credited to the user's Smart Money work-earnings record during the applicable period. Pending, unapproved or estimated amounts are not treated as credited earnings. See the Refund Policy for the complete conditions.
How do I request a refund?
Contact the official support team after the 30-day period and provide the registered account details, package activation date and payment reference. The team will verify the relevant account and earnings records under the published policy.

Referrals & support

Are referral commissions guaranteed?
No. Referral commissions, where applicable, depend on the current written program rules, verified transactions, account status and eligibility conditions. A displayed percentage is not a guarantee of payment.

For payment/login questions, use smartmoneycoustomar@proton.me. For general enquiries, use smartmoneyhelpcare@gmail.com. Never include passwords or one-time codes.
