Privacy & Policy

Read how Smart Money may process account, payment-support and technical information.

Effective date: 26 October 2026. This page explains, in plain language, how Smart Money may handle information when you visit the website or use an account. Update this policy if your actual data practices change.

1. Information that may be processed

Depending on the features you use, Smart Money may process account details such as your name, email address, mobile number, referral identifier and profile information that you choose to submit. For purchases or package access, relevant transaction references, package selections, payment status and activation records may be processed to verify the request and provide support. Do not send passwords, one-time codes, banking PINs or full payment credentials by email.

2. Technical and usage information

The hosting provider, authentication service or database service may process technical information needed to deliver the site, secure accounts, prevent abuse, diagnose errors and keep services reliable. This can include browser/device information, request logs, timestamps, IP-related technical data or error details, depending on the provider and configuration. The exact information collected depends on the services enabled on the live website.

3. Why information is used

  • To create and authenticate accounts.
  • To display profiles and package access correctly.
  • To verify payments, resolve activation issues and respond to support requests.
  • To maintain website security, prevent misuse and investigate technical errors.
  • To communicate important account, policy or service updates.
  • To meet applicable legal obligations where required.

4. Service providers

Smart Money may rely on third-party infrastructure, such as website hosting, authentication, database/storage or payment providers, to operate particular features. Those providers process information under their own service terms and privacy practices. Information should only be shared with a provider to the extent needed for the feature or service. Review the relevant provider policies when using a feature that depends on an external service.

5. Payments

Payment information may be handled by the payment method or provider used for a transaction. Smart Money should retain only the transaction details needed to confirm payment, manage package access and respond to disputes, rather than asking users to send sensitive banking credentials. A screenshot sent to support should hide unrelated balances, account numbers and other private details.

6. Cookies and local storage

The site or its providers may use browser storage, cookies or similar technologies to support login sessions, remember preferences, protect forms, or improve basic functionality. The exact use depends on the deployed code and third-party services. You can review browser settings to manage cookies, but disabling essential storage may prevent login or other site features from working correctly.

7. Security

Reasonable technical and organisational safeguards should be used to protect account and service information. No website or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Use a unique password, protect your email account, log out on shared devices and contact support if you suspect unauthorised access. Smart Money support does not need your password or one-time code to investigate a normal request.

8. Retention

Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably needed for the purpose for which it was collected, including account operation, payment records, support, security, dispute handling and applicable legal requirements. Retention periods can vary by data type and service provider. If you request deletion, some records may need to be retained where required for legitimate operational or legal reasons.

9. Your choices and requests

You may contact support to ask what account information can be corrected, accessed or deleted. We may need to verify that a request comes from the account holder before acting. Some information cannot be changed or removed immediately if it is required to maintain transaction integrity, prevent fraud or comply with legal obligations. We will explain relevant limitations where applicable.

10. Children and appropriate use

The platform is not intended to encourage children to make purchases without appropriate adult involvement. If you are under the age required to enter a binding purchase agreement in your location, involve a parent or guardian before purchasing a package or submitting payment information.

11. External links

The website may link to external services. Smart Money does not control the privacy practices of third-party sites, so review their policies before submitting information to them.

12. Policy updates

This policy may be updated when website features, providers or applicable requirements change. The latest version published on this page will be the reference version. If a change materially affects how information is handled, the website should provide notice where required.

13. Contact

For payment/login privacy questions, the website lists smartmoneysupport1@gmail.com. For general enquiries, it lists Smsupport7@gmail.com. Do not include passwords, one-time codes or full banking credentials in your message.

Important: This is a general website policy draft. It must be checked against the actual data collected, service providers, retention settings and applicable law before being treated as a final legal document.
