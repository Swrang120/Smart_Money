Refund Policy

Learn how to contact Smart Money support for payment problems and refund reviews.

Effective date: 26 October 2026. This page explains how to request a review of a Smart Money package payment. Any mandatory rights under applicable consumer law remain unaffected.

1. Before purchasing

Read the package description, displayed price, available course list, access conditions and Terms & Conditions before paying. Content marked “Coming Soon” is planned content and should not be treated as currently available. If an important detail is unclear, contact support before purchasing and keep the response for your records.

2. When to contact support

Contact support if you believe you were charged more than once, paid but package access was not activated, received access to a different package than the one shown on your confirmed order, or encountered another payment-related problem. Send the registered email address, package name, transaction reference, transaction date and a short explanation. Hide unrelated banking information from any screenshot.

3. How to request a review

  1. Email smartmoneysupport1@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account where possible.
  2. Use a clear subject such as “Smart Money Refund / Payment Review”.
  3. Include your registered email, package name, transaction reference, date and a description of the issue.
  4. Do not include your password, one-time code, banking PIN or full card details.
  5. Keep the support reply and transaction receipt until the issue is resolved.

4. Review and decision

Each request should be reviewed against the transaction record, package access status, the circumstances described and applicable law. Support may ask for reasonable additional information to verify the transaction. A request is not approved merely because it has been submitted; the team should communicate the decision and any next steps. Where a refund is approved, the method and timing may depend on the original payment provider and its processing rules.

5. Duplicate or failed transactions

If your payment app shows a debit but the website still shows a pending status, do not immediately make another payment. First check whether the transaction is pending, failed, reversed or completed. Send the transaction reference to support so the payment can be checked. Some payment providers take time to update the final status. Any confirmed duplicate or incorrectly captured payment should be reviewed and handled according to the payment records and applicable law.

6. Package access and use

If you have already received access to digital content, that fact may be relevant to a refund review, but it does not override any refund right required by applicable law. Do not assume that all purchases are automatically refundable or that all purchases are automatically non-refundable. The outcome depends on the actual circumstances, the published terms at the time of purchase and applicable consumer-protection requirements.

7. Referral or commission records

If a package purchase is associated with a referral record or commission calculation, that record may need to be reviewed when a payment is cancelled, reversed or refunded. A displayed commission example is not a guarantee of earnings. Any adjustment must follow the applicable written program rules and law.

8. Response times

Support response times may vary based on message volume, payment-provider processing and the information available. Please keep the same email thread where possible and reply with any requested transaction details. Sending passwords or one-time codes is never required for a normal payment review.

9. Contact

For payment and login issues, email smartmoneysupport1@gmail.com. For general enquiries, email Smsupport7@gmail.com. The website currently does not show a confirmed support phone number, so use the published email channel unless the site is updated with another official method.

10. Policy updates and legal rights

This policy may be updated when the service or legal requirements change. The version available at the time of purchase and any rights provided by applicable law may be relevant to a request. Nothing in this policy is intended to exclude a consumer right that cannot legally be excluded.

Important: This is a conservative draft. Before relying on it for live sales, the site owner should confirm the exact refund eligibility rules, deadlines and process with a qualified professional and ensure they match the actual payment and package operations.
